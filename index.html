PRESERVATION WITHOUT FICTION

The rules are part of the product.

01 / RIGHTS LEDGER

Old ≠ public domain.

A game only becomes hostable here when there is a defensible redistribution basis: public domain, freeware grant, open license, publisher permission, or another documented authorization.

02 / IMMUTABLE INTAKE

Trust the hash, not the filename.

Hosted titles are built from pinned upstream packages. The materialization job verifies the expected package identity before adding emulator configuration and provenance.

03 / LOCAL MODE

Your copy, your browser.

Commercial and uncertain titles can still receive compatibility profiles without us distributing their data. You provide the copy; the runtime stays client-side.
